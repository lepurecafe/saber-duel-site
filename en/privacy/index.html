Privacy Policy

Your play stays close to you.

Effective: 2026-09-15

Information handled by the app

Saber Duel works without an account. The current app includes no advertising, user tracking, or third-party analytics SDKs. It does not send gameplay data to a developer-operated server.

Motion and nearby play

Motion sensor data is processed on your iPhone to produce swings, sound, haptics, and clashes. During nearby play, devices exchange temporary connection identifiers, blade state, impact strength and timing, connection maintenance messages, and capability information and proximity measurement tokens on supported devices. Distance measurements are processed locally.

Protecting communication

After connection, game messages are encrypted with session-specific keys. Service advertisements used for discovery and the initial key exchange are not covered by this encryption. Automatic pairing does not verify the real identity of another player.

Information stored on your device

Your selected hilt and onboarding completion status are stored on your device. The app itself does not persist gameplay history or raw motion recordings. Deleting the app removes its local data. Data restored through Apple backups depends on your backup settings.

Permissions

Local network access is used to discover and connect to a nearby opponent. On supported devices, Nearby Interaction measures distance to the other device. The app does not access GPS location, the camera, microphone, contacts, or photo library. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings; restricting them may prevent related connection features from working.

This website and Apple services

We have added no analytics tools, advertising, or tracking cookies to this website. GitHub Pages, our hosting provider, may process visitor connection information such as IP addresses for security. See the GitHub Privacy Statement. App Store, TestFlight, and Apple diagnostics are governed by Apple policies and your settings.

Support and deletion requests

If you email us, we process your email address and information you choose to include to respond to your request. We retain it for as long as needed to handle your inquiry. Contact us below to request access, correction, or deletion. Please do not send passwords or other sensitive information.

Contact and changes
